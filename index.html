01

02

03

LEAD RECOVERY

Lost or inactive does not always mean the opportunity is gone.

AdmitFlow AI looks for observable recovery signals in your CRM, such as previous counseling activity, recorded value, recent inactivity and follow-up history.

01

Find Recoverable Opportunities

Surface inactive leads where the available CRM signals indicate that a structured recovery action may be worth reviewing.

02

Give the Team a Clear Move

Instead of simply showing an old lead, provide a practical follow-up direction based on the available evidence.
